nnnnnn## **How to Choose Junior Tennis Rackets**

nnnnChoosing a tennis racket needs great consideration and prior knowledge of all the pros and cons of having them. Here is how we have developed selection criteria based on our experience and the top performers' responses to a racket. 

nnnn### **The child’s Height and length of Racket **

nnnnAll the adult rackets have the same length of 27” but you need an individual estimation before you buy a tennis racket for a junior player. Too lengthy a racket drags on the floor and too short a racket does not let a child learn the skill.

nnnn<figure class="wp-block-kadence-image kb-image1239_9fca11-35 size-large kb-image-is-ratio-size">![The child’s Height and length of Racket ](/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/The-childs-Height-and-length-of-Racket-1024x576.webp)

</figure>nnnnSo as a thumb rule, measure your child’s height first and his racket should be shorter than his legs. Place a racket next to your child and ask him to extend his arm to easily touch the butt cap of the grip. If he does so easily, that’s the size he needs. 

nnnnIf he can’t touch it without bending or stretching the arm, then you need to change the size.
