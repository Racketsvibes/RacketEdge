nnnn### **1. Groundstrokes: The "Rocket Launcher"**

nnnnThe first thing I noticed? **The sweet spot is enormous.**

nnnnEntering 2026, many brands have tried to copy Yonex’s signature "Isometric" head shape, but none have perfected it like this<sup></sup>. The hitting area feels much larger than a standard 100 sq. inch racket.

nnnnOn forehands, the ball explodes off the string bed. I found I could generate deep, penetrating shots even when I was slightly late on my swing<sup></sup>. The 2G-Namd Flex Force graphite snaps back instantly, giving you that "trampoline" effect that pushes your opponent behind the baseline<sup></sup>.+1

nnnn**The Downside:** If you have a very flat swing, you might launch the ball long. This frame is tuned for topspin<sup></sup>.

nnnn### **2. Volleys: Quick but "Twisty"**

nnnnAt the net, the EZONE 100 is fast. The aerodynamic beam cuts through the air, making it easy to get your hands in position for a reaction volley<sup></sup>.

nnnnHowever, stability is the one area where I wish it had more "beef." When I blocked heavy passing shots slightly off-center, I felt some **torsional instability** (the racket twisted in my hand)<sup></sup>. It lacks the rock-solid stability of a heavier frame. But for punch volleys and put-aways? It’s a weapon.

**EZONE 100:** More power, larger sweet spot, easier to use. Best for 3.5-4.5 players.<br><br>**EZONE 98:** More control, thinner beam, requires better technique. Best for 4.5+ players.### **What string setup do you recommend?**
