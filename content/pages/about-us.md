nnnnnnnn## Meet The Founder

nnnn### **Chris Davies**

nnnn**Founder & Lead Gear Tester**

nnnnI am a competitive player, a student of the game, and a total gear nerd.

nnnnFor years, I was frustrated by the lack of transparency in the racket sports industry. Reviews were either too technical to understand or too vague to be useful. I wanted to build a resource that bridged that gap combining data with real-world "feel."

nnnnMy testing philosophy is simple: **"Prove it."**

nnnnI specialize in putting rackets through "stress tests" to answer the questions that actually matter to you:

nnnn
n- **Stability:** Does the handle twist when I block a 100mph serve?nnnn- **Comfort:** Will this frame hurt my arm after 2 hours of play?nnnn- **Power:** Is the sweet spot actually forgiving, or is it just marketing fluff?n
nnnnIf I haven't played a competitive set with a racket, you won't see it recommended on this site.

</figure>nnnnnnnn## How We Test Gear?

nnnnWe don't accept paid placements to "guarantee" a positive review. My testing process is simple and brutal:

nnnn
n- **The Weigh-In:** I measure the *actual* weight and balance (which often differs from what is printed on the frame).nnnn- **The Drill Session:** We hit hundreds of forehands, backhands, and serves to find the racket's "personality."nnnn- **The Match Play:** I use the gear in live games to see how it holds up under pressure.nnnn- **The Verdict:** I rate it based on who it is for (Beginner, Intermediate, or Pro)—not just who pays the most.n
